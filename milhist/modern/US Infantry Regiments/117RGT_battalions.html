4th Battalion

4th Battalion, 117th Infantry Regiment

The 4th Battalion developed from the 4th Armored Rifle Battalion, organized in 1959 from the 170th Armored Infantry Battalion.

Headquartered at Henderson, Tennessee, it took the battalion designation in 1963 and joined the 30th Armored Brigade in 1973. National Guard battalion survey (Wikipedia).

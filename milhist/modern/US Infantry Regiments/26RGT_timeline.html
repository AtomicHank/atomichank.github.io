One regiment, separate assignments

The regiment held the division assignment until 15 February 1957. After conversion to a parent regiment under the Combat Arms Regimental System, its battle groups and battalions held their own assignments. The rows below separate those histories. A deployment or temporary attachment does not, by itself, change the assigned division.

Earlier regimental battalions are covered by the common regimental row. Post-1957 rows follow the CMH lineages of the 1st and 2d Battalions, derived from Companies A and B; they are not a claim that the old battalion organizations continued unchanged.

Scope and source notes

  • 1916–1917: the CMH lineage establishes the 8 June 1917 assignment. A preceding division assignment has not been established here; the opening interval is unverified, not asserted to be independent service.
  • Other battalions: the 3d and 4th Battalions have separate service notes. Their complete assignment dates remain unverified, so they have no continuous bars and are excluded from the date lookup.
  • Training service: TRADOC is a command, not a division. Headquarters activations for training are labeled accordingly.
  • 2008: the CMH record gives 16 March for the 1st Battalion’s brigade assignment. The secondary account gives only March for the 2d Battalion’s inactivation; the chart marks that month as uncertain.
  • 2015: use the official lineage’s effective date, 16 April, for the 101st assignment. An activation ceremony can occur on a different date.
  • Current affiliation: the Fort Campbell directory lists 1st Battalion, 26th Infantry under the 2nd Mobile Brigade Combat Team. Historical entries retain the brigade title used in their source.

41st Infantry Regiment Battalions

Modern Era, United States, Infantry

This page collects the existing battalion illustrations and additional documented historical battalions of the 41st Infantry Regiment. The historical entries include inactive units and earlier designations; the regimental image identifies entries without a battalion-specific illustration.

1st Battalion

1st Battalion, 41st Infantry Regiment

This entry now anchors the battalion page in the regiment's origin or defining early identity, giving the 1st Battalion slot a more specific historical frame than the first scaffold pass.

The 41st Infantry Regiment was organized during World War I and later became associated with the mechanized and armored Army of the twentieth century. Its lineage reflects the transformation of infantry service from foot regiments toward armored infantry and combined-arms warfare.

2d Battalion

2d Battalion, 41st Infantry Regiment

The 2d Battalion entry uses the regiment's middle or operational arc to give the page a clearer sense of how the parent unit developed over time.

The regiment is especially linked to the 2nd Armored Division and later to armored and mechanized service in World War II and beyond. In later decades battalions of the lineage continued to serve in the active Army, carrying the regiment's history into the age of heavy maneuver warfare.

3d Battalion

3d Battalion, 41st Infantry Regiment

The 3d Battalion entry now carries the regiment into its later or enduring modern identity, tightening the page around the way the lineage is remembered in the modern Army.

The 41st Infantry therefore belongs to the modern mechanized tradition rather than the older frontier tradition, illustrating the Army's technological and organizational shift in the twentieth century.

4th Battalion

4th Battalion, 41st Infantry Regiment

The 4th Battalion was formed by reflagging the 2d Battalion, 50th Infantry, in the 2d Armored Division (Forward) in Germany.

This entry records an additional Cold War battalion of the regiment. USAREUR historical compilation.

6th Battalion

6th Battalion, 15th Field Artillery Regiment

The 6th Howitzer Battalion, 15th Artillery, served with the 7th Infantry Division in Korea from July 1960 to July 1963. It returned to active duty at Fort Sill on 1 November 1966 and became the 6th Battalion in April 1968.

It was inactivated at Fort Lewis on 22 November 1969. Its lineage received the designation 6th Battalion, 15th Field Artillery, on 1 September 1971. Army lineage history, Part 1, PDF page 622.

7th Battalion

7th Battalion, 15th Field Artillery Regiment

The 7th Howitzer Battalion, 15th Artillery, was activated at Fort Bragg on 17 September 1962. Its later designation became 7th Battalion, 15th Field Artillery, and it was inactivated at Fort Lewis on 31 July 1972.

A second period of service began at Fort Ord on 1 January 1985, when the battalion was activated with the 7th Infantry Division. It was inactivated there on 15 September 1993. Army lineage history, Part 1, PDF page 625.

8th Battalion

8th Battalion, 15th Field Artillery Regiment

The 8th Missile Battalion, 15th Artillery, was activated at Fort Bliss, Texas, on 19 April 1961. Its headquarters lineage descended from the old headquarters of the regiment's 2d Battalion.

Renamed the 8th Battalion, 15th Artillery, in December 1965, it became the 8th Battalion, 15th Field Artillery, on 1 September 1971 and was inactivated at Homestead Air Force Base, Florida, on that date. Army lineage history, Part 1, PDF page 628.

8th Battalion

8th Battalion, 26th Field Artillery Regiment

Headquarters and Headquarters Battery, 8th Observation Battalion, 26th Artillery, was designated in December 1957. Renamed Headquarters and Headquarters Battery, 8th Battalion, 26th Artillery, in June 1966, it was activated at Fort Sill on 15 July 1966.

The headquarters was inactivated at Fort Lewis on 28 April 1971 and received its field artillery designation that September. The Army's entry specifically documents the headquarters battery, rather than a complete set of organic battalion elements. Army lineage history, Part 1, PDF page 768.

Research note: The added historical entries cite their sources individually. Regimental illustrations are labeled as such. An earlier battalion designation can continue through another battalion or a separate battery; these relationships are identified in the entries. These additions follow Janice E. McKenney, Field Artillery (U.S. Army Center of Military History, 2010), Parts 1 and 2. The published lineages principally cover developments through 2003; they are not a current order of battle.

6th Battalion

6th Battalion, 7th Field Artillery Regiment

The 6th Howitzer Battalion, 7th Artillery, was activated with the Army Reserve's 77th Infantry Division at White Plains, New York, in May 1959. Renamed the 6th Battalion in March 1963, it was inactivated in December 1965.

It received the field artillery designation in September 1971. In October 1987 its headquarters became Battery F, 7th Field Artillery, and was activated with the 25th Infantry Division at Schofield Barracks; that later service was at battery strength. Army lineage history, Part 1, PDF page 478.

7th Battalion

7th Battalion, 7th Field Artillery Regiment

The 7th Howitzer Battalion, 7th Artillery, was activated in the Army Reserve at Worcester, Massachusetts, on 1 May 1959. It served with the 94th Infantry Division until inactivation on 1 March 1963.

The field artillery component became the 7th Howitzer Battalion, 7th Field Artillery, in September 1971. Its designation was shortened to 7th Battalion, 7th Field Artillery, on 15 September 2003; the Army's lineage history lists it as inactive. Army lineage history, Part 1, PDF page 480.

8th Battalion

8th Battalion, 7th Field Artillery Regiment

The 8th Missile Battalion, 7th Artillery, was activated at Fort Bliss on 9 April 1962. Redesignated as the 8th Battalion in June 1965, its headquarters incorporated the former Headquarters Battery, 7th Artillery Group, that December.

The battalion's field artillery and air defense lineages separated on 1 September 1971. The 8th Battalion, 7th Field Artillery, was inactivated at Fort Bliss on that date, while the air defense component continued separately. Army lineage history, Part 1, PDF page 482.

6th Battalion

6th Battalion, 5th Field Artillery Regiment

The 6th Howitzer Battalion, 5th Artillery, served in the Army Reserve with the 77th Infantry Division, with headquarters in the Bronx, New York. Its organic elements were activated on 1 May 1959, and it became the 6th Battalion in March 1963.

The battalion was inactivated in the Bronx on 30 December 1965. The field artillery component received the designation 6th Battalion, 5th Field Artillery, in the 1 September 1971 lineage separation. Army lineage history, Part 1, PDF page 442.

6th Battalion

6th Battalion, 1st Field Artillery Regiment

The 6th Battalion's modern history includes Army Reserve service at Harrisburg, Pennsylvania, as the 6th Rocket Battalion, 1st Artillery, from 1959 to 1963. It returned to the Regular Army in 1967 with the 6th Infantry Division at Fort Campbell and was inactivated there in 1969.

After the 1971 separation of field artillery and air defense lineages, it became the 6th Battalion, 1st Field Artillery. It served with the 1st Armored Division in Germany from 16 January 1988 until 15 January 1994. Army lineage history, Part 1, PDF page 370.

7th Battalion

7th Battalion, 1st Field Artillery Regiment

The 7th Howitzer Battalion, 1st Artillery, was activated in the Army Reserve at Cleveland, Ohio, on 20 March 1959 with the 83d Infantry Division. Renamed the 7th Battalion in 1963, it was inactivated on 31 December 1965.

It returned as the 7th Battalion, 1st Field Artillery, at Chicago on 1 September 1971. Its headquarters later moved to Addison and back to Chicago, where the battalion was inactivated on 15 September 1996. Army lineage history, Part 1, PDF page 372.

8th Battalion

8th Battalion, 1st Field Artillery Regiment

The 8th Missile Battalion, 1st Artillery, was activated at Fort Bliss, Texas, on 24 September 1960. It was redesignated as the 8th Battalion, 1st Artillery, on 30 June 1968.

On 1 September 1971 the combined artillery lineage split. The field artillery element became the 8th Battalion, 1st Field Artillery, and was inactivated on Okinawa; the air defense element continued under a separate lineage. Army lineage history, Part 1, PDF page 374.

9th Battalion

9th Battalion, 1st Field Artillery Regiment

The 9th Howitzer Battalion, 1st Artillery, was activated in Hawaii with the 25th Infantry Division on 1 July 1961. It left that division and was inactivated in 1963, then served at Fort Carson as the 9th Battalion, 1st Artillery, from August 1967 to August 1968.

Its field artillery lineage was designated the 9th Battalion, 1st Field Artillery, on 1 September 1971, when the former antiaircraft component became a separate air defense artillery lineage. Army lineage history, Part 1, PDF page 376.

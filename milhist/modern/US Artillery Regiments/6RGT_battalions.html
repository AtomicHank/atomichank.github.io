8th Battalion

8th Battalion, 6th Field Artillery Regiment

The 8th Howitzer Battalion, 6th Artillery, incorporated the former Battery H, 6th Field Artillery, in September 1963 while serving with the 1st Infantry Division. Its organic elements had been activated on 20 April 1960, and it became the 8th Battalion in January 1964.

It was reorganized as the 8th Battalion, 6th Field Artillery, on 1 September 1971. The battalion was inactivated at Fort Riley on 13 September 1972 and relieved from assignment to the 1st Infantry Division. Army lineage history, Part 1, PDF page 460.

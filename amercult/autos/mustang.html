Ford Mustang

Home / American Culture / Automobiles / Ford Mustang

Introduced in 1964

Ford introduced the Mustang at the New York World's Fair on April 17, 1964. Its appeal combined sporty styling with an accessible entry price and a range of options, allowing buyers to choose different levels of equipment and performance.

The Mustang helped define the pony-car market: a recognizable, youthful shape offered in many configurations. Its cultural importance lies partly in that flexibility. The same name could represent everyday transportation, a carefully personalized possession, or an enthusiast's performance car.

What to look for

  • Sporty proportions with a long hood
  • A broad menu of equipment and options
  • Personal identity expressed through a mass-produced car

Introduced in 1953

Chevrolet presented the Corvette at General Motors' Motorama exhibition in New York's Waldorf-Astoria in January 1953. Production began in Flint, Michigan, on June 30. The first year's run comprised just 300 cars, all with white exteriors and red interiors.

The Corvette brought a distinct sports-car identity to Chevrolet's range. Its compact two-seat layout and fiberglass body separated it visually and materially from ordinary family cars. Following the model across generations shows how a recognizable name can accommodate changing technologies, styling, and expectations of performance.

What to look for

  • Two-seat sports-car layout
  • Fiberglass body in the original model
  • A small initial production run
